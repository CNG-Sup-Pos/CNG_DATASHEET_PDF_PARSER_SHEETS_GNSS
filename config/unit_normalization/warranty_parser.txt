warranty_parser — extraction & normalization rules (years)


Label detection

Match aliases: Warranty, Guarantee, Support, Service, Warranty period, Limited warranty.

Look in footer, legal, or MECHANICAL / GENERAL spec blocks.

Candidate selection

Capture same-line, next 1–3 lines, or nearby paragraph. Vendors often state 2 years, 36 months, 12 months limited warranty.

Tokenization

Extract numeric + unit tokens: patterns like \d+\s*(year|years|yr|yrs|month|months|mo), or \d+\s*months. Include phrases like lifetime support, one year return-to-base.

Canonical mapping

Canonical fields: warranty_years (decimal allowed), warranty_months, warranty_type (limited, standard, extended, lifetime, return-to-base, advance-replacement).

Normalize months → years where appropriate (12 months = 1 year).

Parsing & normalization rules

If X years present → warranty_years: X. If months present convert and store both warranty_months and warranty_years if clean.

If multiple warranties exist (e.g., 1 year standard, 3 years with registration), capture primary as warranty_years: 1 and Notes: 3 years with registration.

Extract support types (phone support, online, extended warranty sold separately) into warranty_type and Notes.

Ambiguity resolution

If vague phrase standard warranty applies without duration, set warranty_years: unknown and Notes: standard warranty stated, duration unspecified.

If limited lifetime present, set warranty_years: lifetime and flag for manual review.

Confidence scoring

High: explicit duration with unit and adjacency to warranty label.

Medium: duration present elsewhere in legal paragraph.

Low: implied or indirect mentions.

Fallback & logging

If no warranty info found, leave blank and append log entry.

Log multi-value conflicts (e.g., different durations in different document sections).

Examples

Warranty: 2 years return-to-base → warranty_years: 2; warranty_type: return-to-base.

One year limited warranty, extended warranty available → warranty_years: 1; warranty_type: limited; Notes: extended available.
